Vyntric is a dedicated Minecraft team of builders, redstone engineers and PvP gladiators.

We started as a group of passionate friends on a vanilla survival world and now operate our own high-performance dedicated server. What began as huddled nights fighting phantoms around a dirt shelter evolved into monumental sky fortresses, automated perimeter factories, and ranked PvP scrims.
